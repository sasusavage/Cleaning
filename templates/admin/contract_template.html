Service Agreement Template

This is the contract text clients will see and sign when booking a contract-based service. A copy is sent to their email after booking. You can edit this at any time — changes apply to future bookings only.

Use plain text. Line breaks are preserved. This exact text is shown to clients at checkout and included in their confirmation email.

How it works

  • When a client selects a contract service at checkout, a modal appears showing this full agreement.
  • They must scroll through it, enter their full legal name, and tick the checkbox to continue.
  • One-time services are not affected — they never see this screen.
  • After booking, the client receives a confirmation email with the signed contract text attached.
  • The admin can view the signed contract text in the booking details on the Requests page.
